# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

KQUANT aims to provide a welcoming, professional and technically rigorous open-source community. Contributors, maintainers and users are expected to treat one another with respect regardless of background, identity, experience level or point of view.

## Expected behavior

Examples of positive participation include:

- giving specific, constructive technical feedback;
- disagreeing with ideas without attacking people;
- documenting assumptions and evidence clearly;
- respecting project safety boundaries and review processes;
- helping newcomers reproduce issues and understand the codebase;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them openly.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, intimidation or discriminatory language;
- personal attacks, insults or deliberately hostile communication;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- intentionally sharing credentials, private financial data or sensitive security details;
- spam, market manipulation, deceptive performance claims or promotion presented as project evidence;
- repeated attempts to bypass the project's research-only and no-execution safety boundaries;
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source project.

## Project spaces

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, documentation, community channels and other spaces used to represent KQUANT.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from project spaces.

For sensitive conduct reports, avoid posting personal or security-sensitive details publicly. Contact the maintainer through an appropriate private GitHub channel when available.

## Scope of technical debate

Strong technical disagreement is welcome. Claims about strategy quality, model performance or financial outcomes should be supported with reproducible evidence and clearly separated from speculation. KQUANT is a research project, not a venue for investment solicitation or guaranteed-return claims.
